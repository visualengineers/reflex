Elastic Displays enable users to interact with complex data through the interactive deformation of an elastic surface. Since the first prototypes emerged more than 20 years ago, research in this area has produced a wide range of concepts and prototypes. However, continuity in development or comparability across systems are very limited. We argue that a key reason for this fragmentation is the lack of a shared technological foundation that can facilitate follow-up work, such as rigorous user studies, complex application scenarios, and standardization efforts.

This paper presents ReFlex, an open framework guided by Open Science principles, which has been designed to serve as a unifying technology platform for Elastic Displays. We present measurements to validate the technological maturity of the framework and techniques to promote collaborative use. Finally, we critically reflect on the practical challenges in implementing such a platform and outline open research questions.
